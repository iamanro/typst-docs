---
title: Research writing
description: Practical guidance for turning a Typst document into a paper that survives review — structure, abstracts, figures, citations, and working with coauthors.
---

Typst handles layout, numbering, and citations for you. It does not tell you what to put in your abstract, how to answer a hostile reviewer, or how to keep three coauthors' edits from colliding. These pages are about that other half of writing a paper: the decisions you make before you ever type `#set`.

Each page stands on its own — read the one you need, in any order.
